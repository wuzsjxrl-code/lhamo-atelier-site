Shipping

Complimentary shipping is included for jewellery orders over $250. Each piece is packed with a care card and storage pouch.

Returns

Unworn ready-made pieces may be returned within 14 days of delivery. Bespoke and personalized compositions are final sale unless faulty.

Stone colour, grain and pattern will vary. These differences are part of the material character rather than defects.

Avoid water, perfume, lotions and ultrasonic cleaning. Wipe after wear and store separately from harder jewellery.
